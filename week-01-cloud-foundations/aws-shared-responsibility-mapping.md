THE SUMMARY
The summary of the shared responsibility model on AWS clarifies wether a customer or amazon is to be hold for ransom; meaning when something goes wrong who is to be held accountable, so AWS is accountable for the the security, functioning of those physical servers, and the customer is accountable for properly configuring, setting limitations and access when using AWS services.

THE SCENARIO ANSWERS
Scenario 1--Exposed S3 bucket: The customer is at fault, reason is; no customer record should be left to the public to view, and it was the customer who made that mistake of making it visible NOT AWS. Scenario 2--Unpatched Ec2: The customer is also at fault, reason is; AWS don't know if you NEED an upgrade but assumes you know what you are doing when you refuses to patch the instance. Scenario 3--Hardware failure: AWS is at fault, reason is; the customer is NOT physically at those data centres to fix hardware issues, and this is NOT inside the cloud But THE cloud itself.

THE REASON IT MATTERS IN BUSSINESS
The reason why this matters in real business is because business is all about profit and finding the source of loss, so if the company always blames AWS for losses then AWS is obliged to remit and pay for it, but it's the company's fault. . .You have to take control
